I am a PhD candidate with [Prof. Dr. Abhinav Sharma](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=qmmD61AAAAAJ&hl=en) at the University of Augsburg in Germany. Prior to this, I completed my dual BS-MS degree at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Pune in India. There, my masters thesis investigated the Thermodynamic Uncertainty Relation (TUR) in open quantum systems under the supervision of [Prof. Dr. Udo Seifert](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=MT2dIvYAAAAJ&hl=en) and [Prof. Bijay Kumar Agarwalla](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=ndIGyi0AAAAJ). I am primarily interested in nonequilibrium phenomena at the interface of physics and biology. I mainly employ tools and techniques from statistical physics at and beyond equilirbrium, and verify my theoretical findings using Brownian dynamics simulations. Outside of science, I am an avid movie goer (a cinephile even) and enjoy playing football, swimming and running - the quarter life crisis trifecta.
